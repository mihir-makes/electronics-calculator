Electronics Toolkit

A collection of small electronic calculators for enthusiasts & engineers

Voltage Divider

Insert Vin and resistor values, find Vout, ratio, current and ADC count. One-One Resistor & Multi Resistor modes.

Reverse ADC Calculator

Insert resistor values and an ADC raw reading, back-calculate the actual Vin at the divider input.

Op-Amp Calculator

Inverting, non-inverting, and difference amp gain/output calculators.
